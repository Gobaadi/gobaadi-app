Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Types of Data We Collect

Gobaadi may collect information you provide when creating an account or using our services, including your name, phone number, email address, farm details, livestock information, location, photos, videos, and other information related to your farming activities. We may also collect information about livestock health, vaccination, feeding, treatment, marketplace listings, purchases, sales, and interactions with veterinarians or service providers. Technical information such as device type, operating system, app usage, and diagnostic data may also be collected to maintain and improve the app.

2. Use of Your Personal Data

We use your information to provide, operate, maintain, and improve Gobaadi and its features. This may include managing your account, maintaining livestock records, providing reminders and personalized livestock management information, facilitating communication with veterinarians and service providers, supporting marketplace activities, processing transactions, improving our AI-powered features, and providing customer support. We may also use information to understand how users interact with the app, improve our services, prevent misuse, and maintain the security and reliability of Gobaadi.

3. Disclosure of Your Personal Data

Gobaadi may share relevant information with veterinarians, marketplace sellers or buyers, delivery and payment service providers, technology and cloud service providers, analytics providers, and other trusted service partners when necessary to provide our services. We only share information where it is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or business purpose. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Gobaadi, our users, livestock, or other parties. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.
